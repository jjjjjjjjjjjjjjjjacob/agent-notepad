# Code of conduct

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project-managed contribution spaces. It covers human participants and the
automation they submit or operate. Product content is also subject to the
separate [contribution skill](skills/agent-notepad/SKILL.md) and
[moderation policy](docs/MODERATION.md).

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect regardless of background, identity, experience, or
  technical ability. Make room for questions and corrections.
- Discuss the work with concrete examples and evidence. Critique ideas and
  implementation without personal attacks.
- Be accurate about authorship, sources, test results, limitations, and the
  behavior of tools or automation you use.
- Respect maintainers' time: search for duplicates, keep reports focused, and
  avoid repeated demands, spam, or manufactured engagement.
- Protect others' privacy. Share only the information needed to explain an issue,
  using sanitized examples and logs.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing,
impersonation, and retaliation are unacceptable. Do not publish credentials,
private conversations, or personal information without authorization. Do not
use issues, code, or review comments to trick people or agents into disclosing
secrets or taking unauthorized actions.

## Report a concern

Jacob is the maintainer responsible for repository conduct concerns. For a
non-sensitive concern, flag the relevant behavior in its project discussion.
For a private concern, ask Jacob for a private contact channel without including
sensitive details; no separate private conduct address is published here.
Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Provide the relevant context and links through the appropriate channel. Avoid
reposting harmful or private material publicly. Reports should be handled with
care for participants' privacy; do not assume an issue or PR is private.

## Response

The maintainer may request changes, remove inappropriate material, limit or
end a discussion, or restrict repository participation according to the
severity and pattern of behavior. Serious abuse may require immediate action.
Responses should be proportionate and explained when doing so does not expose
private information or create further harm.

If you believe a decision misunderstood the facts, provide new context to Jacob
through the same appropriate channel. This project has one maintainer and no
separate appeal committee. Repository conduct decisions do not grant authority
to operate the product's moderation or financial controls.
