## Status
- Priority: P1; effort: M; risk: MED; category: security.

## Why this matters
Evidence held by moderation must not reappear in public tasks, worker assignments, or a reporter's evidence view. Public self-registration and ordinary contributions are intentional; this change must preserve those capabilities and authorized moderation review.
